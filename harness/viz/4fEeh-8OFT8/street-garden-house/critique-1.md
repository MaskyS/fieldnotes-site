# Candidate 1 critique

Material deviations requiring correction:
- The gatehouse front is drawn as an elevation embedded in a top-down plan, with an invented repeated panel count and door hardware. Replace with a single plan frontage line and flush threshold gap; describe panels by label only.
- Dark charcoal vignette fills neighboring areas which were required to remain blank. It is unsupported by evidence and makes title and notes hard to read. Require opaque white paper throughout.
- Continuous gravel wash reads as an asserted exact course and broad garden extent, despite the caveat. Reduce to sparse isolated stipple patches; the connecting course must be dashed and illustrative.
- Gatehouse has a relatively squat symbolic footprint. Its real depth is unknown, so do not fix proportions by assertion; use a visually slender dashed symbol with explicit unverified-footprint label.

Other invented schematic choices, already disclosed: rectangular footprint symbols, relative size and spacing, left-hand tree location, shrub placement and extent, centered entry location and straight street line. These are not surveyed and must remain symbolic, dashed or labelled. Exact plot side edges are invented and unnecessary; omit them. The single small tree symbol represents the catalogue's small tree, not a botanical identification. Main-house entry is a generic grey-blue symbol, not a measured opening.

Faithful: front-to-back relationship, pale gatehouse, groundcover at threshold, gravel and green garden, gallery/study labels without rooms, main dwelling behind, dashed footprint edges, detached 2 m note. No people, scale or north arrow. A second candidate is warranted for the material deviations above.
