# Candidate 1 critique

Viewed candidate-1.png and compared with all four selected film frames.

Material deviations: broad solid outer end panels make the shelves look like two freestanding cabinets, whereas the film shows long built-in runs; closed tops and bottoms invent the termination of these runs. The repeated equal-height bays and sparse, near-identical book groups are more regular than the dense mixed books/discs in the film. These require candidate 2.

Other deviations/inventions: the centered seated pose, bilateral hand gesture, generic dashed seat, shelf spacing and exact cross-sectional placement are diagrammatic inventions, appropriately labelled indicative; they are not a captured moment or measured reach. Five displayed shelf levels are a graphic selection, not an established count. Wood grain and individual book positions are stylized, not transcriptions. The horizontal books are moved to a lower bay rather than copied from the close-up. Gray cast shadows imply an unrecorded lighting arrangement. The illustration uses slight perspective, not a pure orthographic section. Rendering appears transparent/dark outside the drawing rather than opaque warm paper, reducing text contrast. The small touch strokes are partly solid and need visibly dashed connectors. A double-ended shelf label line could resemble a dimensional arrow; replace with separate leaders.

Faithful elements: warm light timber, coloured and white spines, opposing inward shelf faces, tight body/object focus, blank concealed construction, no dimensions or performance claims, and anonymous scale glyph instead of depicted tour participants.
