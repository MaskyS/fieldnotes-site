# Candidate 1 critique

Material deviations: a standing human figure has been copied into the greenhouse despite the prohibition on people. Distinct grey rocks are invented within the pond vignette; the selected pond reference does not establish these. Shelter interior contains interpreted piles/contents that should be left blank. These require candidate 2.

Faithful: tall gabled clear shell and silver frame around ochre timber house; lower level and upper railing; one open timber heap left of one dark lidded cylindrical bin with intervening tree; overgrown pond with concealed water; smaller open-front shallow-roof shelter. All four feature groups have dashed position-approximate locators. Required schematic and non-surveyed captions present; no north, boundaries, approach, continuous paths, tank or treatment arrows.

Illustrative departures: the composition is a pictorial map of separated vignettes rather than a unified elevated survey view, appropriate to missing placement evidence. Tree count, foliage, exact canopy shapes, relative scale, window/mullion spacing and watercolor textures are simplified. Peripheral rocks are contextual from exterior evidence, but should not imply measured positions. Slightly idealized planting and glass reflections are cosmetic. Small incidental greenhouse objects are too detailed and should be softened; no new furniture should be added.
