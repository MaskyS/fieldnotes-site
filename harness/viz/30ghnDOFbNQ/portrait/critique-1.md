## Deviations visible to me

- The painting is substantially brighter overall. The reference has a deeply shaded front elevation, lower level, right-hand approach and foreground; the painting reveals far more in all four areas.
- Sunlit yellow-green flecks spread across the foreground and right slope, while the reference concentrates its strongest visible lawn illumination on the left. This changes the light pattern of the moment.
- The sky is bluer and has invented white brushstroke cloud patterns. The film sky is paler and much more nearly blank.
- Foliage is warmer and yellower. Some leaves inside the right glass facade read as autumn yellow, whereas the vantage reads predominantly green. This weakens the seasonal match.
- The drooping branches retain their broad positions but not their exact silhouettes: leaf clusters are coarser and denser in places, and the central hanging cluster has a different lower edge. Some facade areas are revealed or hidden differently.
- The left foreground trunk is more heavily textured and somewhat broader toward the bottom, with invented warm bark highlights. Background conifer outlines and gaps between trees are approximated rather than copied exactly.
- The greenhouse glazing and frame are too clean, bright and explicit. Reflections differ in shape and position, and several roof intersections, braces and mullion spacings are approximated. The front remains recognizably the same gable, but the fine structure is not an exact transcription.
- The timber facade has stronger golden colour and more evenly legible board courses. Window reflections, divisions and pale frames are sharpened or simplified beyond what the shaded vantage establishes.
- Balcony slat spacing, small objects and the muted blue patch are more specific and brighter than the source supports. A reddish rectangular object on the central railing is particularly conspicuous in the painting.
- The lower left interior is over-described: individual pots, bottles, table-like surfaces and green furniture are much clearer than in the vantage. Dark chair-like shapes and a narrow ladder-like shape in the lower right are also too explicit to assert from that frame alone; supporting views must not license such detail.
- The pale entrance siding, ladder rungs, landing cross-braces and steps are brighter and more precisely articulated than in the vantage; the exact small structural divisions differ.
- The blue car is more conspicuous, with an invented crisp grille, headlights and pale plate shape. Its broad colour and position are retained, but its fine appearance is not established at this level of detail by the vantage.
- The garden shelter interior is too exposed and organized into distinct stacks and boards. Its post and opening proportions are approximate, and the dark roof has acquired clearer seam-like marks.
- The low stones along the right building base have become a more continuous, prominent rocky edge. The foreground has extra individually defined rocks and grass strokes that cannot be matched one for one to the film.
- The isolated left lawn boulder has a slightly different contour and stronger highlights; small ground contours and bare patches are also approximated.
- There is an ambiguous light/dark mark inside the left upper doorway. It is not a clearly depicted person, but should be watched by the judge because the source has no visible people and no figure should be introduced.
