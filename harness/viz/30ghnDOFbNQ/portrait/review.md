The sky has invented white cloud-like strokes and stronger blue colour. The painting brightens the facade, lower level, approach and lawn, spreads sunlight beyond the source's strongest left-hand patch, and shifts some foliage toward yellow. Those lighting and colour changes are deviations from the filmed moment, not evidence of another season.

The painting gives small balcony objects, lower-level pots and furniture-like shapes, shelter contents, car details, entrance joinery and ladder rungs more definition than the vantage supports. An ambiguous mark in the left upper doorway was also noted in the self-critique; it was not intended as a person.

These inventions arose in translating a shaded, partly obscured film frame into a detailed painted image. They were not justified additions from elsewhere in the film.
