# Accepted portrait review

## Chosen round

Round 2, `candidate-2.png`, is the accepted painting. The independent judge reported retention 1.0 after round 1 scored 0.944 on 18 questions. The only requested correction was the van body's main colour from dark gray to gray; round 2 applied that correction while preserving the existing composition and details. Acceptance establishes agreement on the judged questions, not exact photographic fidelity.

## What is faithful

The gouache painting follows frame-0017 at 00:52.5, a whole-subject front three-quarter view of Dave Orton's Ford Transit. It retains the high roof, gray metal body, closed doors, unglazed cargo-side panel, dark grille, cab glazing, headlights, pale front plate and two prominent near-side wheels. The van faces left, with the timber outbuilding behind it on the left and a partially cropped car on the right. The gravel drive, dry yellow grass, green wooded backdrop and bright daylight preserve the vantage's setting and visible conditions.

Supporting catalogue frames frame-0018, frame-0043 and frame-0346 informed exterior materials and trim. Their different viewpoints did not authorize adding hidden camper features. The painting does not expose the transforming interior, bed, bicycle storage or solar-cell surface. The title describes dry-season sunshine without asserting a calendar season that the frame cannot establish.

## What is invented or altered, and why

Gouache rendering simplifies and reconstructs fine detail: individual leaves, gravel marks, reflections, wheel fasteners, headlamp internals, timber joints, flowers and details of the second car are painted approximations rather than verified copies. Some are more prominent than in the film. These arose during image generation and are not additional evidence about the property.

The first round also introduced small geometric and tonal departures: a more exposed far-front tire beneath the bumper, slightly changed van proportions and framing, brighter foliage and larger sky gaps, a stronger roof attachment silhouette, altered shadows, and badge-like markings despite the instruction against lettering. An indistinct pale windshield mark became flower-like; its identity is not established. These are generation errors, not intentional claims about the subject. Round 2 retained them because the judge requested only the body-colour correction and the instruction required keeping everything else.

The gray correction makes the upper roof and side panels lighter than the filmed body may warrant. The accepted colour answer is gray, but the exact paint value is still an approximation. Minor brush-texture changes occurred during the edit; preservation is visual rather than pixel-identical.

The license plate's characters and the source watermark were deliberately omitted to comply with the prohibition on text. No unseen interior, extra building or other property's feature was intentionally added.

## Why this view and round

The selected vantage shows the complete, discreet exterior characteristic of this camper conversion. Full-size inspection showed that the competing side and overhead views crop parts of the van and contain people, contrary to the scout's notes. The nearby frame at 00:54 provides closer exterior detail but crops the vehicle, so it remained supporting evidence.

Round 2 is chosen because it resolves the sole reported failure and was explicitly accepted with retention 1.0. The saved critiques document remaining visual limitations; no further edits were made after acceptance.
