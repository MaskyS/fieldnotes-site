# Candidate 1 critique

Material deviations require a second candidate.
- The remote invents an Inverter ON state, 12.6 V and 0 kW readout, and replaces the observed button arrangement with four buttons. The reference has a different display and six lower buttons. Remove the detailed remote drawing; use a text note that the remote is visible.
- The engine inverter invents a front socket/end pattern and a printed generic 1000W INVERTER label instead of the observed blue label. Simplify to the observed black ribbed side and plain blue stripe, with rating only in the attributed caption.
- Shore plug is rendered with three similar cylindrical gold pins rather than the observed plug geometry. Simplify its perspective so two flat blades and a ground pin are legible; no gold embellishment.
- Solar grid is generalized, not an exact cell transcription; retain only simplified pattern.
- The selectors are given dashed boxes even though the legend reserves dashed blocks for equipment not shown. Remove selector boxes; keep simple controls and labels.
- Selector branch marks look solid while narration-only links must be dashed. Use dashed arrows throughout, ending at destination labels.
- The shower-water arrow appears to lead into the remote inset; remove inset and end arrows at the heat destination text.
- Dark blue-grey wash behind black text severely reduces readability and departs from requested white paper. Use flat white.
- Microwave face proportions are simplified and its door has an overly small grey centre rectangle; use broad dark glass with two right-hand dials. Wood trim and black casing are supported.

Faithful core: one panel, one concealed battery, two separately rated inverters, MPPT route, alternative shore input, and charge through Magnum. Battery/MPPT/Magnum blocks have no invented physical internals. No direct DC vehicle-to-house battery path. All schematic placement, colour coding, generic knobs and graphic spacing are editorial, not surveyed.
