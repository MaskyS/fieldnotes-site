# Candidate 1 self-critique

Compared candidate-1.png with the full-resolution frame-0081.jpg after running the required flatten tool (reported opaque). The reference fixes the view; supporting photographs are material evidence only. This is the first candidate, not an accepted round.

The overall aerial framing and approximate positions and scale are close: the overlapping silver-grey and beige house roofs, reddish porch, small shower to the left, red-roof shelter behind, grassy track to the right, and surrounding forest are retained. No sky, main-roof solar array, chimney, RV, swimming pool, captions or borders have been added.

Visible deviations:

- The painting is appreciably more saturated, yellow-green, bright and contrasty than the damp, grey-green, hazy reference. Distant forest looks too crisp and foreground foliage too luminous.
- The left foreground tree has altered branch routes and a more exposed, heavier dark trunk; its pale upper branches and individually dotted leaves are more prominent than in the reference. Its precise canopy outline and gaps are not faithfully traced.
- Fern crowns, banana leaves, shrubs and foreground trees have been simplified and redistributed. Some large foreground leaf clusters are made more distinct and separated than the dense overlapping vegetation in the frame. Small orange and reddish plant accents are more conspicuous and some are not securely supported at those exact positions.
- The grassy track is too sharply edged, patterned and uniformly legible, with stronger dark-and-yellow surface mottling. Its lower-right course is a little more open and less obscured by foreground vegetation.
- The main roof is more streakily brush-textured and higher contrast. The relative roof planes are close, but the beige strip near the upper ridge is more distinct, and the dark triangular opening between the upper and lower roofs is sharpened and slightly exaggerated.
- The front beige roof looks cleaner and warmer overall despite scattered marks; the reference has finer corrugated lines, differently placed debris and more subdued weather streaks. Small perimeter and roof-junction contours have been regularised.
- The porch is brighter orange-red and the cream wall strip more exposed. Slender posts are darker and more emphatic, while shadows, wall divisions and small openings below the eave do not exactly match the indistinct reference.
- The right-hand steps and their timber edges are more visible and orderly than the partly plant-obscured steps in the frame. The front potted plants are larger and clearer, with altered shapes and spacing.
- The ladder and pale foundation blocks are more conspicuous and crisply separated from the ground. Their exact proportions and small details are approximated.
- The shower bamboo is brighter, more cylindrical and more explicitly banded; its panel edges and grid are too crisp for the reference distance. Small pale utility shapes around the shower have become more definite objects than the vantage warrants, including the white vertical rectangle beside it.
- The red garden shelter is more open and structurally explicit: posts, cross-bracing, base and green interior shapes are clearer than the small shaded forms in the reference. These details should not be treated as established by the vantage. Its roof is brighter pink and its radiating divisions more pronounced.
- The pale rectangular garden object below-left of the shelter and surrounding small plantings have been sharpened and simplified, changing their outlines and local spacing.
- Fine foliage, timber grain, solar-cell lines, roof debris and other tiny marks are painterly approximations, not recovered evidence.

Intentional requested omissions: the source watermark is absent. The small dark human-like figure on the lower-right track is also absent under the no-people instruction; the scout marked this frame people-free, but that mark was not relied on to reproduce the figure. No other viewpoint was substituted, and no hidden interior, vehicle or additional building was deliberately introduced.
