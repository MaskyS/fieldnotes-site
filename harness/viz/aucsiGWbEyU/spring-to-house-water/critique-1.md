# Candidate 1 critique

Viewed candidate-1.png at full resolution and compared it with every generation reference.

- Spring: invented picturesque mound, rock arrangement, exposed pooling stream and prominently projecting outlet. References show an irregular, mostly covered damp patch and a partially obscured small pipe, not this open cascade. Remove visible discharge and pool, reduce to low grass/moss patch with only a small pipe segment. Cover outline can be irregular; internal capture remains blank.
- Tank: recognizable dark wrapped cuboid, hoses and round lid; illustrative proportions and folds are approximate. Lid rendered smaller and more neatly centered than close-up supports; avoid exact port placement and cut exposed hoses short. No internal vessel claim.
- Annex: recognizable housing, pale head, red fittings and yellow bucket. Dense internal grid reads as specified filter construction despite the media disclaimer. Leave the housing interior empty. Simplify valve arrangement, which is decorative approximation rather than a verified plumbing topology; do not assign flow through individual fittings. No water should be drawn into the bucket.
- Countertop: stacked steel body and black tap are faithful in broad form; relative vessel height, tap orientation and lid details are stylized. Keep no brand, internal filter or fill hardware.
- Stove: invented compact equal front doors and hardware do not match the reference's large pale enamel oven body and open side door. Use plain, explicitly functional stove symbol, omitting door count, hinges and handles. Vessel is correctly dashed and reported rather than falsely observed.
- Pump: blocky generic motor shape differs from visible blue motor/grey pump and upper control box. Use minimal blue/grey pump symbol without speculative ports or controls; shelf only if necessary. All transfer arrows remain dashed.
- Seasonal symbols: correctly dashed/unlocated and separate. Summer cylindrical storage shape is a generic convention, not observed geometry; use a plain rectangle to reduce that implication. Winter reserve correctly has no physical construction.
- Flow: long supply links and countertop filling are dashed; however summer/winter inputs float separately without tying to a known supply node. This is acceptable and safer than inventing a branch junction; retain wording that source links are unobserved. No return or waste route exists.
- Typography/meaning: captions, capacities, attribution, times and uncertainty legend are legible. Layout is purely editorial and scale/proportions invented, explicitly nonspatial. No humans are present.

Corrections for candidate 2: remove cascade/pool, blank filter interior, reduce stove/pump to faithful functional symbols, rectangular summer storage, retain dashed uncertainty and attribution. No new film references.
