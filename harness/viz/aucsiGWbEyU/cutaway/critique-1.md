# Candidate 1 critique

Compared with interior reference sheet, historical plan/section 0030–0031, roundwood ceiling 0066, plus additional door 0412 and range 0691 viewed to resolve mismatches.

- All ten legend titles match the catalogue, but callouts 3 and 4 repeat between scene and insets despite the request for unique numbers. Use one numbered target per item.
- The main entrance is incorrectly woven willow: the real shortened entrance is blue-grey panelled joinery with fan-shaped glazing (0412). The woven door belongs to the interior partition. Replace exterior woven door with the blue-grey panelled fan-glazed entrance, and keep willow only in a detached detail inset.
- The house has been made nearly circular and symmetrical; historical 0030 shows flattened opposite sides with curved ends. Preserve a rounded-ended footprint, not a roundhouse. Exact footprint remains dashed and schematic.
- A solid continuous rear wall and bedroom partition are invented, despite the note 'Unseen geometry omitted'. Erase those walls to blank paper and dashed outline; do not put solid openings into unseen rear geometry.
- Cordwood is installed on the far-right outer wall, unsupported and inconsistent with its filmed relationship beside the entrance. Remove it from the building and retain only the separate inset, labelled as seen beside entrance; position is not inferred from its inset placement.
- The metal range is a generic dark cast-iron stove with glass-style doors and small sidebox. Reference 0691 shows a rectangular range with worn pale blue-grey oven front on the right and dark brown firebox at left. Correct the appearance and omit unshown sidebox/details.
- Kitchen cabinet weaving is too finely regular and the layout/shelf lengths are invented; use broad irregular brown bark strips like 0487, and mark overall placement schematic. Jar count, books, tiny tools, kettle and decorative plants are not individually grounded by selected references; omit excess clutter.
- The bed footboard, paper lamp geometry, exact tatami extent and all furniture distances are extrapolated. Simplify and retain only visible branch headboard, blue blanket, blue platform and pale mats; unresolved bed end dashed.
- Roof resembles an unbroken flat plank roof with an arbitrary rear dotted outline, and has no meaningful missing-layer band. Show only a lifted fragment of shallow sloping planks/round rafters; missing layer band blank/dashed above, not a claim of exposed planks as exterior finish.
- Flue location and roof penetration are invented. Stop the solid flue below removed roof; draw a dashed continuation labelled 'Connection / penetration not shown'.
- Post count, braces and structural joints are excessively specific; reduce to representative observed roundwood and mark hidden joints blank/dashed.
- Edge vegetation, foundation border stones, solid cut wall cap layers and window divisions add unverified details. Omit vegetation and base stones; simplify cut edge, and state 'Cut geometry schematic'.
- Toilet inset has a solid full box with no visible separate shavings compartment. Use the reference close-up composition: blue top, wooden oval seat and metal bucket; indicate unshown compartment with a dashed outline and note.
- No people or installed bath are invented. The palette and major materials are broadly correct, but uncertainties need visual treatment rather than only a general subtitle.
