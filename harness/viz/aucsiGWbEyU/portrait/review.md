# Vosges homestead, winter sunlight

Round 1 (`candidate-1.png`) is the chosen painting. The user reported that the independent judge accepted it with retention **1.0**. That result records agreement on the judged visual questions; it does not establish exact fidelity in every detail. No further round was made.

The sole vantage is `review/aucsiGWbEyU/evidence/frame-0102.jpg`, at **348.666667 seconds**. This people-free winter aerial was chosen because it shows the complete main-house elevation together with the uphill annex, greenhouse and solar-panelled shed. Catalogue frames 0040, 0019, 0142 and 0244 supplied closer material references; they were not intended to authorize additional objects or another viewpoint.

The painting faithfully retains the broad downward viewpoint, absence of sky, winter light and bare trees. The brown earthen main house remains on the right, the separate annex uphill and left, the low greenhouse between them, and the red-tiled shed with solar panels at lower left. The turf roofs, timber posts and diagonal braces, one main-house metal flue, principal window and tall-glazing arrangement, corrugated shed extension with a dark collector, and cropped blue vehicle remain recognizable in their source positions. There are no people, labels, insets, borders, pond or solar panels on the main house.

The gouache brushwork is an intentional invention of medium. The following are remaining departures from the evidence, rather than verified property details:

- The annex has an unsupported small side window. An indistinct roof object and the pale object outside its doorway become overly definite, including basin-like fittings.
- The shed solar array merges middle module boundaries. Panel grids, collector divisions, hoses, roof weights, stored objects and tile patterns are simplified or altered.
- The main house has warmer, cleaner render, more regular timber braces, invented pale post pads and a conspicuous stone-like foundation band. Frames, sills, the threshold, flue and tiny marks behind the glazing are more definite than the vantage supports; the rounded end is slightly squared off.
- Greenhouse frames and roof turf become more regular, and some source occlusion is lost. More of the blue vehicle is exposed than in the reference.
- Branches, shrubs, ground vegetation, soil beds, paths and shadows are approximated, with changed contours and warmer colours. The painting does not preserve their exact geometry.

These departures appear to come from the generated painting simplifying irregular forms and resolving ambiguous pixels into specific objects. Influence from supporting views is a possible explanation for the annex window, but its cause is not established. None of these additions was required by the vantage rule, and this review does not treat them as evidence. The detailed visual record remains in `critique-1.md`.

Round 1 is retained because it was explicitly accepted after judging, while the limitations above remain documented. Hidden interiors, the spring and water tank, and unseen building sides were outside the intended depiction. No image changes or publication steps were performed for this acceptance review.
