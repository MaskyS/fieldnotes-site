# Candidate 1 self-critique

Compared the full-size vantage `frame-0102` at 348.666667 seconds with the generated PNG, and viewed `comparison-1.jpg` with the vantage on the left and candidate on the right. The comparison image only resizes the painting for inspection; `candidate-1.png` is the original generated output. Medium: gouache, generated with the built-in image tool. This is a self-review, not the independent judge result.

## Visible deviations

- **Solar module boundaries:** the tiled-roof array loses the clear separation between the two middle modules, which read as one broad module in the painting. The outer module geometry and cell-grid divisions are also changed. The original separate rightmost module is retained.
- **Annex side:** the painting gives the shaded right side of the uphill annex a small, definite rectangular window. That opening is not discernible in the vantage; the supporting close-up must not license it.
- **Annex roof:** a small mauve-grey, flat object near the back of the roof is too crisply delineated. The vantage does not resolve its outline or identity reliably. The roof turf becomes a more orderly, lumpy mound, and the fascia is brighter and more regularly outlined.
- **Annex doorway surroundings:** the pale curved object in front becomes a clearly shaped basin with distinct fittings, and adjacent stones and supports are sharpened into specific forms beyond what the vantage resolves. The dark doorway itself remains in the correct position.
- **Main-house facade:** the brown render is warmer, more orange and more uniformly clean. The low pale foundation becomes a conspicuous continuous scalloped band of stones; the vantage has a subtler, irregular pale base. The right end looks slightly more squared off.
- **Main-house supports:** the posts and braces are straighter, thicker and more neatly forked. Their feet acquire distinct rounded pale pads. The source's irregular timber, partially hidden bases and occlusions are simplified. The partly obscured far-left bay becomes more legible than the film warrants.
- **Main-house openings:** window frames and the tall glazed opening have brighter, thicker blue-grey outlines, more strongly defined sills and invented tiny interior marks. The small right window projects more noticeably. The step below the tall glazing becomes an isolated rounded stone.
- **Main-house roof and flue:** the roof vegetation becomes an even band of golden tufts; the dark rear roof edge is more graphic. The flue cap and lower bend are rounded and sharply defined, with a simplified cast shadow.
- **Greenhouse:** the pale-blue glazing becomes a cleaner, more regular row with thicker golden frames and more opaque reflections. Its right end is somewhat less obscured by the foreground branches.
- **Shed and extension:** the tiled roof is more orange, with an overly regular tile pattern. The mauve doors, gable-side dark opening, green box and stored objects become cleaner and more specific. The corrugated roof weights are larger, rounder and differently distributed; the black hose loops are simplified, and the collector's frame and internal divisions change. The wall cladding is more neatly delineated.
- **Vehicle crop:** more of the blue vehicle's right side and a small side detail are exposed. The reference shows a narrower cropped roof segment. Its contour, roof grooves and shade of blue differ.
- **Vegetation and terrain:** many tree trunks, forks and branch positions are approximated rather than preserved. Bare twigs are thicker and brighter, with especially conspicuous new branch patterns at the right edge. The central shrub mass is more open, golden and legible, with altered outer contours. Background leaf litter is more orange and foreground grass more yellow-green; some dabbed ground vegetation looks leafier than the winter reference.
- **Ground beds and shadows:** dark soil patches are drawn as more continuous, organized strips, while the right-hand path is clearer and wider-looking. Exact grass/soil boundaries and tree shadows have shifted. The strong diagonal shadow on the right and branch shadows on the wall are simplified and intensified.

## Broad features retained

The painting keeps the downward aerial view with no sky, the main house on the right, uphill annex behind and left, low greenhouse between them, tiled solar shed at lower left, cropped blue vehicle, bare winter trees, turf roofs, one main-house metal flue, and the main visible opening arrangement. It introduces no people, text, border, inset, pond or solar panels on the main house. These retained features do not excuse the deviations above.
