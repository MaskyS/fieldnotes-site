# Candidate 1 critique
Major error: callout 5 identifies hanging frying pans as black hot-water vessels. These are different objects: vessels are visible behind upper exterior glazing (0054); cookware hangs in the corridor (0064). The requested separate vessel inset was omitted. Rear wall and floor are solid mottled concrete-like cut sections despite concealed construction; roof detail shows a definite three-layer shell and thickness not established by film. The schematic rear room is disproportionately wide, with an unproven tube/door position relationship and a definite roof junction. Precise timber joinery, bolt count, PV module segmentation, continuous front roof geometry and raised roof box are reconstructions, not inspected assemblies. Vault internal finish, timber glazing, planted corridor, square-framed corrugated inlet and catalogue titles are broadly supported. The drawing conflates a transverse section with a lengthwise greenhouse perspective; the end-wall door and planter give a false precise relationship. Fruit count, stone paving layout and plant positions are invented. Dashed buried tube route is properly qualified but still adds an unsupported elbow. Claim boxes are attributed. No tires were invented, and the single-prototype scope is explicit.
