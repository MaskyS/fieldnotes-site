## What is invented or changed, and why

The gouache treatment introduces brushwork and interpretive surface texture. It also makes the scene brighter, warmer and more saturated than the film, with stronger blue sky, whiter clouds, yellower ground and more vivid plaster and foliage. These are generation deviations, not evidence of different lighting or a different season.

Fine branches, shrub silhouettes, stones, grasses and cloud edges are approximate painted patterns rather than exact reproductions. The bare tree is too thick and prominent, and the foreground contains more emphatic stones and plant detail. Small window divisions, roof projections, wall contours, exposed timber shapes, porch objects and the vehicle are sharpened or regularized beyond what the vantage clearly resolves. The right house's side shoulders appear more rocky and faceted. These details arose from the model's interpretation of soft film detail; they should not be treated as verified construction information.
