# Candidate 1 critique
The four separate views are close to their source frames and do not invent a unified layout. The amphitheater is stretched into a nearly straight regular row; original tiers curve toward the camera and have more irregular stacks, soil gaps and tread levels. Tire count, tread pattern, plant locations and complete rightward extent are invented repetition despite dashed endpoints. Bottle yard is recognizable but table bottles are tidied into a lineup, some brands/labels are fabricated, and the title names paired bricks although the selected frame primarily shows whole loose bottles; clarify that this is preparation context, not a detail of a finished brick. Complex facade preserves the pointed door, can-end texture, exposed tires and sloped glazing; generated circular patterns, opening sizes, curved parapet edge and exact tire arrangement are approximate. Stair close-up preserves rough can-ended riser but regularizes can spacing and invents fine cracking. Extents beyond the photo need blank/dashed continuation rather than completed architecture. No completed river, orchard, apartments or common house is invented. Future work is correctly identified as proposed.
