# Candidate 1 critique

Material deviations requiring correction:
- Stage 2 shows the recessed exterior handle on the upward-facing inner surface, near the cabinet. It belongs only to the exterior and should be hidden underneath in this view.
- Stage 2 attaches the moving panel near the cupboard shelf instead of retaining the bottom edge pivot at the same height as stages 1 and 3. This shortens the apparent moving panel and misrepresents the motion.
- Side-arm lines have small solid attachment marks, implying fixings not established by the evidence. Remove these marks and leave attachment zones blank.

Other deviations and simplifications:
- The repeated viewpoint is synthesized from different film camera positions; it is not measured.
- Wood is lighter and more uniformly lit than the film but retains its warm brown appearance; grain is illustrative, not a traced pattern.
- The microwave controls are stylized; exact button positions should be omitted to avoid false detail.
- Incidental contents and people are omitted deliberately; this isolates the requested transformation.
- The steel foreground is simplified, without a tap; it remains contextual.
- Exact arm geometry and intermediate angle are invented schematic proposals, appropriately dashed and labelled indicative.
- The right-side pale background is simplified from the stair/wall edge and need not be elaborated.

A second candidate is required for the wrong moving-panel arrangement and invented handle placement.
