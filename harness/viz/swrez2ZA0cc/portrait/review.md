# Morris homestead, leafy summer

Round 1 (`candidate-1.png`) is the chosen painting. The user reported that the pipeline accepted it with retention **1.0**. No further round was made. This score records retention on the judge's visual questions; it does not establish exact fidelity in every painted detail.

The vantage is `review/evidence/frame-0057.jpg`, at **194.527667 seconds** in property film `swrez2ZA0cc`. It was chosen as the widest clear, person-free leading aerial showing the complete house roof and frontage together with the pool, planted terraces, driveway and wooded setting. Supporting catalogue frames `frame-0063`, `frame-0225`, `frame-0399` and `frame-0250` clarified materials and details already visible from that vantage. The painting was generated with the built-in image tool using `prompt-1.txt`; the visual questions were written before generation.

The painting is faithful to the elevated aerial view, leafy season, absence of sky and overall arrangement: the green-roofed house and lower left wing, two gridded solar arrays and three smooth dark panel groups, one chimney, pale rendered walls, brown wooden balcony, curved stone terrace and lower stone frontage with three windows and a doorway. It retains the pink umbrella, turquoise rectangular pool to the left of the reddish raised vegetable beds, foreground tree occlusion, left driveway and small grey rear shed. It adds no people, windmill, pond or remote property features. Interiors and features hidden beyond this view remain outside the painting.

Invention occurs primarily in the gouache surface and in details that the distant frame cannot resolve. Individual leaves, crop forms, stones, driveway marks and pool ripples are painted approximations, not observed records of those exact shapes. Roof seams and panel grids are regularized. Window muntins, balcony patterns, grilles, furniture, pots, flowers, hose coils and straw-like objects are more explicit than the vantage supports; closer references influenced their rendering too strongly. The rear play structure and shed are also simplified and more sharply defined.

There are fidelity limitations beyond texture: foliage is brighter, yellower and more saturated; wall and timber colours are warmer; the house and roof proportions drift slightly; tree silhouettes and gaps change some occlusions; paths, retaining-wall curves, pool edges and garden spacing are approximate. Fine wires and branches are partly lost. These differences arose during the generated translation into gouache and are not claims about the real property. The source watermark was deliberately omitted to satisfy the prohibition on text. The detailed observations remain in `critique-1.md`.

Round 1 was retained because the pipeline accepted its question-level visual retention and the user selected it. Its acceptance does not erase the documented inventions or deviations. The chosen image is unchanged.
