# Candidate 1 critique

Viewed the generated candidate against all selected frames.

- The dark green-gray mottled background was invented and makes labels and dashed arrows hard to read; use flat ivory.
- The tank has invented metal lifting handles, a neat exposed base, an altered squat proportion, and fully composed surrounding terrain. Frame 0487 has loose green cover sheets, timber resting behind them, a taller front face and partial earth burial. Remove handles, terrain, and inferred base; use only the visible faces and cover.
- The wind wheel is regularized into a fully frontal complete wheel, while 0456 is oblique and occluded; the tower is rotated into an upright assembled-looking form instead of the lifting orientation in 0458. Use an oblique wheel fragment and a tilted lattice fragment with dashed cropped ends.
- The filter is too squat and generic; the platform becomes a low pallet with invented timber blocks. Use the taller egg-shaped navy vessel, black pump and valve, and simple cropped wood support of 0496. Omit support geometry that cannot be matched.
- Filter timing was invented as 33:06–33:42. Use narration 33:21–33:47 and visual 33:42.
- The gravity arrow starts at the filter, incorrectly implying the house is downstream of the filter in an established series. Start it at the tank and keep it conceptual.
- Section label “at ground level” is unsupported and contradicts the exposed tank wall. Delete it. The solid wall strokes imply thickness; keep the section explicitly schematic and unscaled, using single strokes.
- Section bracket does not label its bottom endpoint as bedrock; add a text-only endpoint. Foundation geometry remains appropriately blank.
- Barn roof is reconstructed from a more frontal viewpoint, adding regularized siding and lower openings. Use only a cropped aerial roof/gable fragment, with obscured portions blank or dashed.
- Shed eave includes inferred regular timber edging. Simplify to observed corrugated roof and wall fragments.
- Node positions, colors used for arrows, panel borders, sketch projections and component sizes are editorial inventions. They are acceptable only as explicitly indicative diagram conventions.
- The blank conventional-well node, lack of well-to-tank arrows, planned collection arrow, conceptual circulation arrows, reported depth attribution, unknown volume and potability caveat are faithful.
