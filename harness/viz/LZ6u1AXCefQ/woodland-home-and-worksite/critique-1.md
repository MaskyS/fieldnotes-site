# Candidate 1 critique

Viewed candidate-1.png against all seven film frames.

- Main house: invented second roof ridge/hip-like roof geometry behind front gable; gable pitch is too steep, facade too square and symmetrical, viewpoint too frontal. Black flue appears freestanding/roof-mounted rather than emerging through right wall. Sliding doorway looks closed instead of partly open. Upper membrane has invented lettering; timber divisions and exact log/bottle patterns are fabricated. Window/door proportions and stone base are simplified. Remove lettering, lower roof pitch, preserve oblique cropped facade and wall-mounted flue, omit unseen roof extent.
- Woodland: invented individual tree positions, dense decorative foreground plants, rocky foreground and track-like ground strip. The title leaf sprig is decorative invention. The reference supports green woodland, trunks, grass and soil only; use soft sparse foliage/ground washes without a path or identifiable species motif.
- Cabin: turned into a taller, tidy, narrow gabled cottage with horizontal siding, invented porch furniture/rail, prominent chimney, symmetrical opening and approach path. Reference shows a low broad weathered gray-green structure partly obscured by trees, broad rusty shallow porch roof, natural posts and higher roof mostly hidden. Crop/leave blank where hidden; no newly completed upper gable, chimney, furniture or path.
- Porch: exaggerated tall triangular gable and regular square-sawn posts; reference has irregular natural posts, low roof and a screened enclosure left of the cabin/open porch. Invented furniture and opening geometry. Interior screen study is too regular and square. Use the observed oblique left screened enclosure and rough wood members; omit objects hidden by people.
- Sawmill: invented exposed motor, wheels, carriage base and neat parallel rail support system; red uprights too tall and heavy. Reference has a lighter red frame with rounded red component and broad beige covering obscuring mechanism. Keep machinery behind covering blank; no restored hidden mechanical detail. Slabs too neatly milled/stacked.
- Cordwood: invented roofed storage shed, continuous supported canopy, repetitive upright structure and uniform short pieces; film shows irregular exposed stack tops, loose rusty sheets and timber weights, rough boards and thin green stakes. Render cropped irregular stacks with loose coverings, not a shed.
- Overall: all insets remain unlocated; required labels and acreage caveat are present. No boundary, compass, mapped watercourse or inter-inset route appears. Main drawing is a perspective locator rather than a surveyed map, appropriate to the limited evidence. Painterly colors, composition, object counts and fine textures are approximations. Footnote accurately states unknown locations but is too broad to excuse reconstructed hidden shapes.
