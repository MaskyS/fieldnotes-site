# Candidate 1 critique

Viewed against all nine inventoried film frames.

1. Major geometry deviation: three projecting logs on the right point along the wall rather than through its thickness. They contradict the top logs and the laying reference. Remove them entirely; every log axis must be parallel to the same front-to-back direction.
2. The 16-inch arrow is horizontal along bay width, not along a log axis. It is a false dimension placement even though labelled reported. Move it directly alongside a top log in the depth direction, or use a leader note without a dimension arrow.
3. The exterior-mortar label points to the rear strip, also labelled interior. Point exterior to the near strip surrounding visible front end grain, interior to rear strip only. Remove redundant interior label at lower right, which points toward the wrong face.
4. The right cut surface reads as a fibrous solid vertical panel with wrongly oriented logs emerging from it; replace with a dashed schematic cut edge consistent with the top core. Clearly identify reconstruction. Sharp solid edges on some hidden interfaces should become dashed.

These are illustrative, not copied or measured. Reduce conspicuous concentric rings; the evidence has rough sawn irregular end grain, not consistently sharp rings.
6. Core color closely follows loose shavings, but its saturation and apparent packing imply more certainty than available; mute the texture and retain the dashed-boundary and narration caveat. Lime content is spoken, not visually proven in the close-up.
7. The representative panel omits bottle glass, openings, setting and construction apparatus; the bottle omission is disclosed and the other omissions are appropriate to scope. No people is correct.
8. Stone face, pale mortar and timber colors are broadly faithful. Blank base interior avoids claiming stone through the full thickness. Post base and below-grade work remain blank. The unseen timber joint is labelled but appears tightly specified; keep the contact as an undetailed white seam, without hardware or claim of an air gap.
9. Splashback and 16-inch facts are correctly attributed as stated/reported; no performance claims were promoted. The label density is excessive and repeats the log length and interior bead labels; simplify.
