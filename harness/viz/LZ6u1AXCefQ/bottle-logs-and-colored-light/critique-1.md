# Candidate 1 critique

Viewed candidate-1.png at full delivered resolution and compared with all nine evidence frames.

- Dark gray/blue atmospheric background contradicts requested white paper and severely reduces text contrast. Colored glows imply an unobserved lighting environment.
- Exploded bodies are elongated and nearly identical straight cylinders; frame 0311 shows shorter, somewhat shaped cut blue bodies. Perspective, separation distance, exact mouth/base profiles and glass thickness are constructed, not measured.
- Sleeve is rendered as a precisely split C-section. Its lifted/open form is an explanatory invention; the film shows a wrapped sleeve. Mark opened edges dashed and label opening diagrammatic.
- Assembled inset shortens the silver middle relative to frame 0308, omits visible gray bands, and shows a light arrow ending inside the sleeve, weakening the transmission explanation. Gray bands should not be assigned a fastening purpose.
- Blocked inset depicts an open mouth facing the viewer rather than clearly showing a closed outward glass face. Glass depth is invented; leave depth blank/dashed. Post is correctly dashed, but its exact size and contact are only inference. Arrow should terminate at the dashed post boundary.
- Motif is a composite with invented insert counts and placements, a single row of blue instead of the broader multi-row band in 0110, and generalized green groups rather than a traced wall elevation. Its schematic label is appropriate. Added red dish in motif is unnecessary mixing of separate views.
- Barrel is invented as a wooden stave tub with metal hoop; frame 0098 shows a tall brown weathered container with blue rim, not visible staves. Replace with a few loose bottle examples, explicitly omit container.
- Molded green face is stylized with incorrect eye/lip modeling relative to 0093; simplify to a recognizable round molded face with broad eyes, pronounced nose and lips, without pretending it is exact.
- Red dish gains elaborate starburst/segmented decoration not established in 0102. Remove this detail. Its polygon shape is also too few-sided in motif; use a plain approximately octagonal red face.
- Bottle bases gain invented stippling and precise ring details; remove.
- Duplicate plural label over each of two bodies is confusing. Label each as one cut body.
- Bottom notes are too small and low contrast. Make unknowns clearly readable.
- Overall composition, leaders, arrows, isolated views, exploded geometry, simplified wood grain and shadows are illustrative inventions, not film observations. No dimensions, seals or screws are drawn; no insulation claim or actual water system is asserted; no people are drawn.
