# Candidate 1 self-critique

Compared the generated gouache directly with full-size frame-0250 at 21:03.5. This is a self-review, not the independent judge's result. No second candidate has been generated.

The principal arrangement survives: the same low oblique front view, one large two-part left window, two narrow right windows, closed dark central door and wreath, two window boxes, two entry lanterns, two canopy braces, right-hand stair rail, timber-edged black stairs and deck, bird feeder, two chains, slatted fence and patchy lawn. No person, solar panel, tank or chimney was added.

Visible deviations:

- The nearly featureless pale overcast sky has become conspicuous blue-gray and cream cloud patches. This invents cloud structure and changes the quiet diffuse atmosphere.
- The palette and local contrast are stronger: the fence is more saturated orange, timber edges more golden, greenery brighter and dark/light edges harder. The frame is softer and more subdued.
- Background trees are not individually faithful. Several trunks and branches are heavier, their spacing and branching differ, and the upper-right foliage is denser and more emphatic. The source has finer, sparser twigs and more open sky between trunks.
- Lawn marks look like scattered broad leaves or flat green patches instead of the source's fine grass tufts. Bare brown areas, green patches and small foreground debris do not follow the source precisely. The slope remains recognizable, but the ground texture is invented.
- Fence slat spacing, individual post positions and right-side receding angles drift. The exaggerated black gaps and wood knots add detail not resolved in the frame.
- The house and roof geometry is close but not exact. The left roof tip sits slightly higher, roof-edge and canopy profiles are thicker and more irregular, and the dark raised roof strip behind the canopy is more pronounced. The painting exposes slightly different amounts of the canopy's pale underside.
- White wall panel seams have become much more conspicuous and regular. Visible surface texture and joint detail exceed what the vantage resolves.
- The dark left masonry pier has more explicit, larger block joints. Corrugation spacing and highlights in the black skirting are regularized instead of copied exactly.
- Window reflections have been repainted into clearer, differently arranged trees and a more legible reflected gray building. Their geometry and tonal pattern do not match the frame. The rightmost window's yellow patch is broader and more distinct.
- Both doorway lanterns appear warmly lit in the painting; the source does not show that conspicuous yellow glow. Their shapes and apparent sizes also vary slightly.
- The door's glass/panel pattern and handle are sharper and more explicit than in the source. The wreath has different leaf shapes and a slightly altered outline.
- The canopy chain and left-corner chain are thicker, brighter and more copper-orange; their individual links are exaggerated. The left chain's course and attachment details drift slightly.
- The bird feeder has slightly altered cap/base geometry and a more defined seed texture. The hook and attachment assembly are heavier, and the small hook-side light/detail is brighter than in the source.
- Flower-box foliage and blooms are larger and denser, with more conspicuous pale flowers. Individual plant shapes and colour distribution are invented rather than matched.
- The conical shrub left of the stairs is broader and more stylized, and its pot is more exposed and explicitly faceted. The small white pot's reddish leaves are enlarged; its outline and overlap with the lower dark chain pot are changed slightly. More right-side shrub detail is resolved around the railing.
- The stair edges and deck top are brighter and slightly thicker. The deck face has more explicit vertical plank joints and a somewhat altered front-corner outline. Stair/door widths and the handrail's posts, cap and internal divisions drift slightly.
- The small black landing stand is clearer and has a simplified outline that does not exactly reproduce the source.
- Right-hand stepping stones are larger, whiter and more sharply separated, and do not follow the source's exact outlines or spacing. The small green strip along the far-right fence is darker and more prominent.
- Gouache brush marks necessarily replace photographic texture throughout; their specific marks are invented. These stylistic marks do not justify the structural or lighting differences listed above.
