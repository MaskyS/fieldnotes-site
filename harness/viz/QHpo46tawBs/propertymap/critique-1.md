# Candidate 1 critique

Viewed against all inventoried frames. The wetland is wrongly a blue ornamental pond with dense reeds and flowers; the current evidence shows gray gravel and sparse reed clumps. The parking outbuilding is invented as a flat-roofed two-story-looking box instead of the observed shallow green pitched roof and closed green doors. The cluster is made into an almost closed horseshoe, with exact invented roof counts, pitches, solar coverage and facade details; the archival model supports only an elongated stepped arrangement. The common house is falsely identified with a detailed tall gabled residence from the pool aerial; its relationship to the circular lawn is observed, but the illustrated building identity and position are unsupported. The lawn contains an invented central tree, and its geometry is too perfect. The pool is rounded rather than rectangular; the roof machinery is simplified into invented equipment. Cistern top, full shape and connection are unobserved. The rain basin is incorrectly a rock-edged ornamental pond with invented flowering plants. Exact trees, saguaros, road curves, footpath network, fence geometry and terrain are illustrative inventions. Dashed paths help, but several unregistered features remain solid and confidently placed. The inset text repeats a conditional instruction instead of stating uncertainty. No people appear, and roof/adobe colors broadly match.
