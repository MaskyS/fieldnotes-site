# Candidate 1 self-critique

Compared the full vantage frame-0258 (881.466667 s) with candidate-1.png after generation. This is my visual review, not the independent judge's result. No second candidate has been made.

The oblique downward viewpoint, framing without sky, housing on the right, covered parking on the left, two far parking outbuildings, foreground scrub wash, and broad path arrangement are substantially retained. The image reads as a gouache painting. No people, words, borders, insets, or annotation marks are visible.

Visible deviations and unsupported specificity:

- The palette is warmer, brighter and more saturated. The original has more subdued grey-green desert vegetation and cooler, darker shadows; the painting adds yellow-olive foliage and orange-tan earth. This changes the impression of vegetation condition despite retaining a dry, sunny scene.
- Foreground shrubs are too neatly separated and repeatedly star-shaped, resembling uniform agave or yucca clumps. The film shows more irregular fuzzy scrub and intermingled groundcover. The exact plants, branching and gaps are invented brushwork, not faithfully copied shapes.
- Several saguaros, especially the conspicuous ones left of the foreground path and around parking, are larger, cleaner and more emphatic than their counterparts in the frame. Their arm shapes and shadows are not supported at this precision.
- Tree canopies between and below the houses are simplified into discrete faceted crowns; the frame has denser, softer and less separable masses. Gaps through that canopy and what they reveal have shifted.
- The central curved path is broader, brighter and more cleanly edged. Foreground forks and narrow trails are more legible and in places wider or displaced. Small pale clearings are enlarged.
- The two nearest buildings retain their placement and major roofs, but their facade divisions, balcony railings, window shapes and visible wall areas are over-regularized and too crisp. Vegetation screening of their lower levels differs. These details exceed what can be read from the vantage.
- Some roof intersections, small projections, little raised gables, vents and seams are simplified or shifted, including the complicated roof between the parking path and the nearest left building. The repeated small distant roofs are more regular than the photograph.
- The dark rectangular panel patch on the left-facing upper roof surface of the nearest left building is not faithfully rendered as the visible panel in the frame; it becomes an ambiguous dark roof shape. Other small roof rectangles have different proportions, and panel grids are sharper than the vantage warrants.
- The right-middle pergola/slatted roof and the little green open frame farther right are too white or bright, clean and distinct. Their nearby paving and planting edges are more orderly than the source.
- The parking lot keeps the broad canopy layout, but some canopy lengths, angles and gaps differ slightly. Supports and vehicles are made much more explicit; exact car outlines, colours and counts at this resolution should not be treated as verified matches.
- The two rear parking outbuildings have exaggerated neat green rectangular door/front divisions. Their overall placement is preserved, but the painting supplies facade detail not clearly resolved in the frame.
- The enclosed patch below the left edge of parking has become an orderly garden of little beds and distinct plants. The frame supports an irregular enclosure and pale ground, but not all these crisp beds, rows, fence divisions or plant identities. Its blue strip is also more saturated and prominent.
- Distant roads and buildings are sharpened and locally reshaped. Individual background houses, roofs, vehicles and driveway edges have been interpreted beyond the source detail; some appear enlarged or more colourful.
- Cast shadows are harder edged and have different small silhouettes, especially around isolated desert plants. The large dark band below the nearest houses is broken into more separate shapes.

The source watermark was deliberately omitted as requested. Gouache brush texture is an intentional stylistic invention. Supporting views were used only as material/form references, but the more explicit architecture, cacti and garden details above remain unsupported specificity and should be evaluated against the vantage alone.
