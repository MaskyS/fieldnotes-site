It retains the green pitched metal roofs, pale walls, shaded verandas, two prominent nearest building blocks, tree canopy among the houses, staggered parking canopies with vehicles, two outbuildings beyond parking, and the broad winding-path arrangement. The distant road and scattered buildings remain in the background. No people, text, labels, borders or insets appear, and interiors or underground systems have not been exposed.

## What is invented or altered

Gouache brushwork and matte painted texture are intentional stylistic inventions. The source watermark was deliberately omitted as requested. Other differences are limitations of the generated painting, rather than verified property details:

- Colours are warmer and more saturated, foliage more yellow-olive, and soil more orange-tan than the frame. Small cast shadows are harder and differently shaped.
- Shrubs become repeated, separated spiky forms; cactus arms and some cactus sizes are over-defined. Tree crowns, canopy gaps and screening of building bases differ.
- Paths and clearings are brighter, wider or locally displaced, although their broad arrangement is retained.
- Window divisions, veranda railings, vents, roof seams, small roof intersections and outbuilding doors are made more precise or regular than the vantage supports. Some solar-panel shapes differ, including an ambiguous dark patch on the nearest left building.
- The pergola and small green open framework are too crisp and bright. Parking canopy dimensions and gaps vary slightly; vehicle details are interpreted rather than individually verified.
- The enclosure below parking has acquired overly orderly beds, plants and fence divisions. Distant roofs, houses and driveways are similarly sharpened and locally reshaped beyond the visible evidence.

These details arose from painterly simplification and the image generator resolving small or obscured forms. They should not be treated as measurements, exact species identifications, exact counts of small objects, or evidence of hidden structures. The full visual deviation list remains in `critique-1.md`.
