# Candidate 1 critique

Viewed against inventory. Critical identity error: the upper cistern copies the green collection-tank access riser with red lid and purple boxes, not the silver corrugated house-side rain cistern. An arrow incorrectly connects infiltration to that cistern. Remove the cistern panel entirely to avoid conflating storage with wastewater collection. Rainfall arrows are conceptual, not observed flow; they need an explicit legend. A new downspout, idealized matching house fronts, young shrubs and bare paths invent current appearance; current paths are densely shaded. Underground infiltration soil section invents stone distribution and profile. Treatment beds are miniaturized raised trays with exposed black liner all around; the evidence shows broad ground-level gravel and local liner exposure in an excavated bed. Exact reed count, bed dimensions, riser scale and vegetation are schematic. The pump is called underground though its precise installation is not shown. Septic and pump blanks and dashed wastewater arrows correctly mark hidden components; no people or closed return-to-potable loop appears. Lower riser is faithful in color and corrugation.
